# Apex Financial Group
## Company Overview

Apex Financial Group is a mid-sized financial services organization with approximately 500 employees operating across Los Angeles, Dallas, and New York.

The company provides wealth management, investment advisory, retirement planning, and financial consulting services to individual and corporate clients.

To support business growth, Apex Financial Group has adopted a hybrid workforce model that includes on-site employees, remote workers, contractors, and third-party vendors.

Because the organization handles sensitive financial information, strong identity and access management controls are required to reduce security risks, support regulatory compliance, and protect customer data.

## Current Challenges

- Decentralized identity management
- Weak password policies
- No Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)
- Manual account provisioning and deprovisioning
- Excessive user permissions
- No Single Sign-On (SSO)
- Lack of Privileged Access Management (PAM)
- No formal access review process

## IAM Transformation Objectives

- Centralize identity management using Active Directory and Microsoft Entra ID
- Implement Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)
- Enforce Multi-Factor Authentication
- Deploy Single Sign-On across business applications
- Automate user lifecycle management
- Secure privileged accounts
- Implement identity governance controls
- Align with Zero Trust security principles
- Improve compliance and audit readiness

## Regulatory Requirements

- **SOX (Sarbanes-Oxley Act)** - Financial controls and separation of duties
- **GDPR** - Protection of EU client data
- **GLBA** - Financial privacy requirements
- **SEC Regulations** - Investment advisor compliance
